before you boot

the chapter
  1. Get the ISODownload 4.0.2 and put it on a USB stick with balenaEtcher (Mac, Windows) or caligula (Linux).
  2. Turn off Secure Boot and TPMIn your BIOS. The installer needs both off.
  3. Choose where it goesThe whole drive, or the free space next to Windows for a dual boot.
  4. Back up firstEverything is encrypted by default, and a full-disk install wipes the drive.

Dual boot and unattended installs have their own chapters.
